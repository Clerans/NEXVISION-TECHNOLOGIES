# Content Plan — Copy Strategy & Structured Architecture
## NEXVISION TECHNOLOGIES — Editorial Framework & Page Copy Blueprint

---

### 1. Brand Voice & Communication Rules
* **Tone:** Authoritative, clear, sophisticated, human, and technically sound.
* **Prohibited Patterns:** No generic AI fluff (e.g., "Revolutionize your paradigm with cutting-edge synergy"), no fake client quotes, no exaggerated stats (e.g., "99.999% profit increase").
* **Key Copy Pillars:**
  1. **Engineering Rigor:** "We write clean, robust code that stands the test of high traffic, complex data, and rapid business evolution."
  2. **Product Strategy:** "Technology built with clear commercial purpose and intuitive user workflows."
  3. **Long-Term Partnership:** "From Day 0 architecture through continuous deployment and iterative scaling."
